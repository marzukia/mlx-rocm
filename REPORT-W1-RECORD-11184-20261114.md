## Mechanism (the zero-point fix)

The fused in_proj QuantizedLinear constructor quantizes an mx.random.uniform weight, so its group zero-points were PER-PROCESS RANDOM. The fusion overrode weight + scales only; the zero-points are a real GEMM input (acc += s*la + b*lx per group) that the layer-0 npz dumps never captured - the named cause of the per-process non-repro (the ~17-member outcome pool was an artifact of the zero-point randomization, not a kernel race). Fix = one line in the model file: fused.biases = the concat of the true group biases from the separate b/a layers. At M=27 (S=27 prefill) the GEMM takes the per-row qmv_wide kernel; at M=3 (S=3 decode) the deterministic qmv_wide_mr3 kernel. The step-2 fp8 pin (below) is INERT on this record path (the fp8 gate requires M >= 64; the RDNA4 dequant crossover for K=4096 / N=64 is 28 > 27). The S2 headline (P 128.42 / D1 93-99.6%) was measured on the CORRUPTED random-zero-point numerics = an artifact; the true-affine numerics are the TPK 2.248 K2 class (a small real fusion win at equal acceptance) and the P form at TPK 2.297.

## The fp8 pin (step-2)

vendor/.../gemms/hipblaslt_gemm.cpp: during stream capture the fp8 GEMM picks the top heuristic (res[0]) instead of the timing autotune - the autotune picks a different algo per process under load, which diverges run-to-run output. Other shapes keep the timing tune (numerics unchanged by scope). This is the cross-process determinism for the fp8 path; it is INERT on the record path (M >= 64 gate).
